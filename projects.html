  • Industrial automation · Team of 3

  • Analog electronics · Solo

    Heart-pulse amplifier

    Project for Electronic Technology, which I built on my own in December 2025: a TCRT5000 infrared sensor picks up the pulse from a fingertip, a high-pass filter removes the DC component, an LM358 amplifies it with a gain of 1000 and a low-pass filter cleans up the noise. The signal goes from millivolts to volts, checked on an oscilloscope and with an Arduino as a low-cost scope.

    • Op-amp
    • Filters
    • Oscilloscope
    • Arduino
